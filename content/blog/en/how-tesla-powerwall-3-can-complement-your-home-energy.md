Having energy available at home is not only about generating it. It also depends on how you can store, manage, and use it when you truly need it.

In homes with solar panels, an important share of energy is produced during the hours of strongest sun exposure. However, a home’s energy needs do not always align with those hours.

That is why adding a storage system such as **Tesla Powerwall 3** can help you make better use of available energy and gain greater control over household energy use.

## Generating energy is only one part of the system

Solar panels can produce energy during the day, but a home’s energy use changes constantly.

In the afternoon and at night, air conditioning, lighting, appliances, and other equipment may be running just as solar generation begins to decline.

Storage makes it possible to keep some of that available energy for later use instead of relying only on generation in the moment.

## What does Tesla Powerwall 3 do?

**Tesla Powerwall 3** is a battery designed to store energy and make it available when the home needs it.

When it is properly integrated with a home energy system, it can help manage when energy is stored and when it is used.

This allows generation, storage, and consumption to work together in a more coordinated way.

## More backup during power interruptions

Another function of Powerwall 3 is to provide energy backup when the electrical grid is interrupted.

Depending on the system configuration, stored energy may be used to keep certain home equipment and functions operating during an outage.

Rather than producing additional energy, the battery provides a reserve for those moments.

## Solar panels and storage have different roles

Solar panels and a battery such as Powerwall 3 do not perform the same job: **they complement each other**.

Panels generate energy from the sun, while the battery stores some of that energy for later use.

Together, they can create a system with more flexibility to decide how and when to use the energy available at home.

## A solution that depends on each home

Every home has different needs. Energy use, peak-use schedules, solar-system size, and backup requirements are some of the factors to consider before adding a battery.

Choosing a storage system is therefore not only about installing a battery. It is about understanding how energy is used in the home and determining which setup may work best.
