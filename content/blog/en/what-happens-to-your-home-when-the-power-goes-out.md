An electrical outage may last a few minutes or extend for hours. In either case, many everyday household activities depend directly on having power available.

From lighting and internet access to certain appliances and essential equipment, a power outage can quickly disrupt your routine.

That is why more and more households are considering systems that let them have stored energy available when the electrical grid is no longer available.

## Energy available when the grid fails

Tesla Powerwall 3 is a battery designed to store energy and use it later in the home. One of its functions is to provide backup power during electrical grid outages.

When an outage occurs, the system can detect the interruption and begin supplying stored energy, helping keep the home loads included in the system configuration operating.

Powerwall 3 has **13.5 kWh of energy capacity**, providing an energy reserve for times when the grid is unavailable.

## How much can a Powerwall 3 back up?

There is no single duration for every home.

Backup time depends on factors such as energy use, the equipment that remains operating, the amount of stored energy, and the configuration of each installation.

For example, prioritizing lighting, refrigeration, internet access, and other important equipment can help manage available energy during an outage.

## More than reacting to an outage

Energy storage helps prepare a home for situations in which relying only on the grid can become a limitation.

The key is to design a system suited to the energy characteristics and needs of each property.
